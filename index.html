06Questions

Before you start a project.

We build AI and LLM integrations, fintech systems, software on demand (dashboards, portals, APIs, mobile apps) and security reviews. If your idea sits between those, send the brief anyway.

We would rather tell you honestly that something is not a fit than take it on and under-deliver.

It runs in four steps. You share the problem through the project form, we review it and reply with questions or a plan, we agree features, price and timeline together, and then we build.

You see progress while we build, not only at the end, and we ship once the agreed scope is done.

We keep pricing affordable and fair. It depends on scope, so it is agreed after we review your brief and before any work starts. The budget field in the form starts from under $50, so small projects are welcome.

Nothing is final until we both agree the scope in writing, which keeps surprises out of the build.

Speed is the reason Software-on-Demand exists. Modular infrastructure, admin dashboards and web portals built to a tight spec can move from brief to launch quickly because the scope is clear and decisions are fast.

Larger AI or fintech systems take longer, and we give you a realistic timeline in the scope step rather than a number that sounds good.

Yes. Mention it in your brief and we are happy to sign an NDA before you share anything sensitive, especially for source code audits, security reviews and financial systems.
